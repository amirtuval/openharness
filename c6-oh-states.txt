==============================================================================

1. A divider that arrives LIVE, while the history under it is already written
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------
   Session opened in `oh -s <id>` with the history on screen, then a manual
   `/compact` run from the shell (the same event a `/compact` in `oh` sends).
   The clipboard below is `tmux capture-pane -S -3000` around the divider.

   ─────────── conversation summarized · manual · openai/gpt-4 · 1 pass ───────────
   History to summarize:
   Fact A: the codename is BLUEHERON-42. lorem ipsum dolor sit amet …
   …

   (Before the C6 fix the divider was never drawn here — `<Static>` cannot
   insert a block that belongs above what it has already written, and the last
   settled message was written a second time instead. See the regression test
   `TranscriptView › draws a divider that arrives once the history under it is
   already written`.)

3. `oh settings` — defaults, validation, set
   -----------------------------------------
   $ oh settings
   Summarize at 10% of the context (the server default).
   Summary model: same as the chat.
   Summary pass limit: 3 (default).

   $ oh settings --threshold 5
   oh: --threshold needs a share between 0.3 and 0.95 (0.7 is 70%), or 'default'.

   $ oh settings --summary-passes 99
   oh: --summary-passes needs a whole number between 1 and 10, or 'default'.

   $ oh settings --threshold 0.3 --summary-passes 5
   Saved.
   Summarize at 30% of the context.
   Summary model: same as the chat.
   Summary pass limit: 5.
